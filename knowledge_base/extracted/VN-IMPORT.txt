 Legal Basis of the Procedure 
 
 
 Customs Law No 54/2014/QH13 dated 23/06/2014 

 - Decree No. 08/2015/ND-CP dated 21/01/2015 of the Government detailing a number of articles of the Customs Law on customs procedures for goods electronic export and import trade. 

 - Circular 38/2015/TT-BTC dated 25/03/2015 specified electronic customs procedures for export and import trade. 

 - Circular 172/2010/TT-BTC dated 02/11/2010 specified for fees and charges for customs. 
 
 
 
 
 Processing time 
 
 
 - No later than 08 working hours for partly physical examination of the shipment 

 - No later than 02 days (working) for fully physical examination of the shipment

 Process Steps: 

 
 
 
 
 

 

 

 

 Step 1: 

 Customs Declaration Submission 
 
 
 1.1: Registration 

 Importers must submit customs declaration to Customs System by IDA. Registered information will be stored and can be modified up to 7 days. 
 
 
 
 
 1.2: Information Change 

 In case of changing information from IDA importer has to go through IDB to change information (if any) 
 
 
 
 
 1.3: Submission of Final Declaration 

 Importer sends final registered custom declaration by IDC 
 
 
 
 
 

 

 

 Step 2: 

 Receiving feedback from Customs System 

 

 
 
 
 2.1: Feedback for Green Channel 

 If Customs System sends feedback for Green Channel importer has to pay duty (if any) and get custom clearance. 
 
 
 
 
 2.2: Feedback for Yellow Channel 

 If Customs System sends feedback for Yellow Channel importer has to submit the following documents for Customs verification 

 (i) BL 

 (ii) Packing list 

 (iii) Goods value declaration form 

 (iv) Import License (if any) 

 (v) Invoice 

 (vi) Specialized Inspection Certificate (if any) 

 (vii) Certificate of Origin (if any) 
 
 
 
 
 2.3: Feedback for Red Channel 

 If Customs System sends feedback for Red Channel importer has to submit the above seven documents and cargo for physical examination. 

 2.4: Importer pays duty and charge/fee 
 
 
 
 
 Step 3: 

 Receiving Customs Clearance 
 
 
 Importer receives Customs clearance
