TRADEX — FIVE-MINUTE DEMO FROM ZERO

Start: npm run demo:empty
Open: http://127.0.0.1:5174/
Restart the command to create a fresh session. Keep the terminal running.

00:00–01:10 — Build the business from evidence.
Show zero orders and an unknown cash balance. Upload or use Read the sample
file, then confirm the three TXT files in preload/, in their numbered order. The folder name describes
the baseline records; do not pre-import them before this demonstration.
Say: "We start with an empty workspace. Our finance officer adds the cash
schedule, the sales order, and the supplier's payment terms. TradeX reads
the fields, and we confirm them before they become the business baseline."

01:10–01:40 — Reveal the problem.
Build the dashboard. CNY 128,000 is available; CNY 160,000 is due on October
22, before the expected buyer receipt on October 29. Show the CNY 32,000 gap.

01:40–02:30 — Introduce the new email.
Open Harbour Supplies, add live-upload/04_buyer_partial_payment.eml in
Documents, wait until searchable, and choose Ask AI. The email promises
partial payment but omits both amount and date. Show the agent's questions.

02:30–03:30 — Human clarification and confirmation.
Supply USD 6,000 and CNY availability on 2026-10-29. Keep the remaining
USD 6,000 unscheduled. Confirm the proposal. The cash forecast updates;
the bank balance does not. Show the peak gap increasing to CNY 34,000.

03:30–04:35 — Compare actions.
Ask the assistant to compare supplier extensions of 10 and 26 days,
holding other receipts fixed. A 10-day extension leaves the CNY 34,000 gap;
26 days avoids a negative balance in this scenario, conditional on later
collections and supplier agreement. Request a negotiation draft.

04:35–05:00 — Close the loop.
Show the updated dashboard and the email citation. Close with:
"TradeX connects the document, the missing information, the cash impact
and the next action. The owner confirms business changes while AI helps
make the work easier to understand and follow through."

Timing includes short interactions; rehearse with the configured live model
before recording. Nothing in the baseline importer is presented as live AI.
