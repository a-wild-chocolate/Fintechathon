               Q1. What is GPU-As-A-Service (GaaS)?
               GPU-As-A-Service (GaaS) is an new services offered by High Performance
               Computing Centre (HPCC) and consists of 25 Compute Nodes with 200 Nvidia
               H200 Cards.
                
               To get the latest updates on what is happening to the Cluster, please subscribe to
               the NTU HPC Users' Group

               Q4. Where can I get sample Submission Scripts?
               A High-Performance Computing Cluster has a large collection of powerful servers
               to process the job. When you submit a job, the Scheduler find the most suitable
               server according to your submission script parameters and place your job there. 
               Sample PBS Script can be found at /usr/local/templates/. Feel free to copy any
               scripts to your directory and modify them as you wish.

               Q9. How do I run a multi-GPU / multi-node PyTorch job?
               PyTorch distributed jobs on the cluster use NCCL over the GPU fabric, launched by
               mpirun from HPC-X (NVHPC 26.3). A single submission script
               (PYTORCH_MULTINODE_TEST.pbs) works for both single-node multi-GPU and
               multi-node runs — only the select= line changes.
               Sample scripts are at /usr/local/templates/. Copy them to your directory and edit
               as needed.
