[JP-DOCUMENTS-B0015] 1107 Documents to be Submitted for Import Clearance (FAQ) : Japan Customs
(1) 
 A Bill of Lading (B/L) is a document issued by a shipping company to a sender acknowledging that they have received the shipment of goods. It is a necessary document for consignee to receive the shipped goods which have arrived in Japan from the shipping company.

[JP-DOCUMENTS-B0016] 1107 Documents to be Submitted for Import Clearance (FAQ) : Japan Customs
(2) 
 A Sea Way Bill is a contract between a carrier and a sender for sea transportation of goods and it is issued when the carrier has accepted the goods and obligated itself to carry the consignment.

[JP-DOCUMENTS-B0017] 1107 Documents to be Submitted for Import Clearance (FAQ) : Japan Customs
(3) 
 An Air Way Bill is a contract between a carrier and a sender for air transportation of goods and it is issued when the carrier has accepted the goods and obligated itself to carry the consignment.

[JP-DOCUMENTS-B0018] 1107 Documents to be Submitted for Import Clearance (FAQ) : Japan Customs
(4) 
 An Invoice is a document describing the name, quantity, and value of the goods, and is prepared in the shipping country of the goods.
