[SG-PERMIT-B0008] Obtain a Customs Import Permit
To import goods into Singapore, you must submit a Customs Import Permit through TradeNet.

[SG-PERMIT-B0009] Before You Apply
Before You Apply

[SG-PERMIT-B0010] 1. Register for a Unique Entity Number
1. Register for a Unique Entity Number

[SG-PERMIT-B0011] 1. Register for a Unique Entity Number
You must first register with the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) ↗ (opens in new tab) or the relevant Unique Entity Number (UEN) issuance agency ↗ (opens in new tab) to obtain a UEN.

[SG-PERMIT-B0012] 2. Activate Your Customs Account
2. Activate Your Customs Account

[SG-PERMIT-B0013] 2. Activate Your Customs Account
After receiving your UEN, activate your Customs Account with Singapore Customs. This is required before any import permit application can be submitted.

[SG-PERMIT-B0014] How You Can Apply for Import Permits
How You Can Apply for Import Permits

[SG-PERMIT-B0015] How You Can Apply for Import Permits
Once your Customs Account is activated, you may choose one of the following options:

[SG-PERMIT-B0016] Option A: Appoint a Declaring Agent
Option A: Appoint a Declaring Agent

[SG-PERMIT-B0017] Option A: Appoint a Declaring Agent
A Declaring Agent (DA) can apply for customs permits on your behalf.

[SG-PERMIT-B0018] Option B: Apply for Permits Yourself
Option B: Apply for Permits Yourself

[SG-PERMIT-B0019] Option B: Apply for Permits Yourself
If you prefer to make declarations yourself or for your clients, you must:

[SG-PERMIT-B0020] Option B: Apply for Permits Yourself
Register as a DA, and

[SG-PERMIT-B0021] Option B: Apply for Permits Yourself
Apply for a TradeNet User ID ↗ (opens in new tab) to access the system.

[SG-PERMIT-B0022] How to Submit Permit Applications
How to Submit Permit Applications

[SG-PERMIT-B0023] How to Submit Permit Applications
All import permit applications must be submitted through TradeNet, using one of the following channels:

[SG-PERMIT-B0024] How to Submit Permit Applications
TradeNet front-end software from an approved software vendor ; or

[SG-PERMIT-B0025] How to Submit Permit Applications
Government Front-End Application ↗ (opens in new tab)

[SG-PERMIT-B0026] How to Submit Permit Applications
These channels allow you or your DA to process import declarations and receive approved permit notifications.

[SG-PERMIT-B0027] After You Apply
After You Apply

[SG-PERMIT-B0028] After You Apply
Once your permit is approved, follow the conditions stated on the permit for cargo clearance and payment of duties and GST (where applicable).

[SG-PERMIT-B0029] Ready to apply?
Ready to apply?

[SG-PERMIT-B0030] Ready to apply?
Submit your application through TradeNet using your preferred front-end channel.
